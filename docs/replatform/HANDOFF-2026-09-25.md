## 7. The one lesson worth carrying

Every blocker this week was the same shape: **something green was answering a question it could not
see.** A guard reading the file you just wrote can only say it is well-formed, never that it is
complete. A gate can demand evidence from a surface that does not exist. CI proves nothing about the
paths it structurally cannot reach. A control that cannot red proves nothing, and neither does a probe
that cannot distinguish.

Sections **E, E.1, E.2, E.2.1, E.3, E.3.1, E.3.2** of `agent-rules/BUILD-RULES.md` are each one
instance of that, generalised. They are worth more than this handoff.
